FBI Detains Passenger Following Mid-Air Altercation on Qatar Airways Flight to Chicago

A Boeing 777-300ER arriving from Doha was met by federal authorities at O'Hare International Airport after a reported physical confrontation between two passengers.

AVIATION BY AARAV NEWSROOM · 2026-11-17 · 1 SOURCE · SINGLE-SOURCE ANALYSIS

At a glance

A physical altercation between two passengers on a Qatar Airways flight from Doha to Chicago resulted in the suspect being taken into custody by the FBI upon landing. The flight continued to its destination without diversion.

Incident Overview

On Monday, November 16, 2026, a mid-air altercation occurred aboard Qatar Airways flight QR725, a Boeing 777-300ER traveling from Doha (DOH) to Chicago (ORD). According to reports from Simple Flying, which cites AirLive, a passenger seated in row 32 allegedly attacked another passenger in row 31. The situation required intervention from both the cabin crew and fellow passengers to de-escalate the conflict.

Operational Response

The flight crew notified Chicago air traffic control of the incident prior to arrival, requesting that law enforcement and FBI agents meet the aircraft at the gate. The flight, which had a total duration of approximately 14 hours and 38 minutes, landed at Chicago O'Hare at 1:46 PM local time. Qatar Airways confirmed that the incident did not impact the safety or operation of the flight, which arrived as scheduled.

Law Enforcement Action

Upon arrival at Terminal 5, the suspect was taken into custody by federal authorities for questioning. Law enforcement officials also gathered statements from passengers who witnessed the event. While Qatar Airways acknowledged that two male passengers were involved in the altercation and were handed over to authorities upon landing, the airline has not provided further details regarding the specific nature of the charges or the identity of the individuals involved.

Key facts

  • Qatar Airways flight QR725 landed at Chicago O'Hare at 1:46 PM on November 16, 2026.
  • The incident involved a physical altercation between two male passengers seated in rows 31 and 32.
  • The FBI and local police met the aircraft at the gate to take the suspect into custody.
  • The flight did not divert and arrived at its destination as scheduled.

What this means

The decision by the flight crew to continue to the destination rather than diverting suggests that the cabin crew successfully contained the threat, and the pilot determined that the safety of the aircraft and its occupants was not compromised. The involvement of the FBI indicates that the incident is being treated as a federal matter, likely due to the flight's status as an international arrival into the United States.

What we could not verify

The details of the altercation rely on reports from Simple Flying and AirLive; these accounts have not been independently corroborated by official law enforcement statements.
