Zero Gravity Unveils ZG-T6 Tiltrotor eVTOL Concept

The Chinese manufacturer targets a 2028 service entry for its six-passenger electric vertical takeoff and landing aircraft.

AVIATION BY AARAV NEWSROOM · 2026-11-11 · 1 SOURCE · SINGLE-SOURCE ANALYSIS

At a glance

Zero Gravity Aircraft Industry has introduced the ZG-T6, a tiltrotor eVTOL designed for passenger and cargo operations, featuring distributed electric propulsion and a planned 2028 market entry.

Design and Configuration

The ZG-T6 is a tiltrotor eVTOL concept currently under development by Zero Gravity Aircraft Industry. The airframe utilizes a carbon fiber composite cabin designed to accommodate one pilot and five passengers. A notable design feature is the inclusion of panoramic wrap-around windows intended to improve visibility for occupants. The aircraft employs a fixed tricycle wheeled landing gear system and utilizes a fly-by-wire flight control architecture.

Propulsion and Performance

The aircraft is powered by a distributed electric propulsion (DEP) system, which utilizes six electric motors to drive six tilt-propellers. These rotors are strategically positioned on the ends of the V-tail, high wing, and low forewing to facilitate both vertical takeoff and landing (VTOL) and forward flight. According to the developer, this configuration is intended to eliminate critical points of failure, thereby enhancing onboard safety through system redundancy. While specific battery details remain undisclosed, the aircraft is estimated to achieve a cruising speed of 250 kph (155 mph) and a flight duration of approximately 90 minutes.

Operational Targets and Certification

Zero Gravity Aircraft Industry has outlined an ambitious timeline, with the ZG-T6 expected to enter commercial service by 2028. The company is currently pursuing certification through the Civil Aviation Authority of China (CAAC). The aircraft is designed to support both urban air cargo missions and passenger transport. Furthermore, the manufacturer has indicated that the platform is being developed with planned autonomous flight capabilities.

Key facts

  • The ZG-T6 is a tiltrotor eVTOL designed for one pilot and five passengers.
  • The aircraft features a distributed electric propulsion system with six motors and tilt-propellers.
  • Estimated performance includes a 300 km range and a 250 kph cruising speed.
  • The developer is targeting a 2028 entry into service with CAAC certification.

What this means

The ZG-T6 represents a growing trend in the eVTOL sector toward multi-passenger, tiltrotor configurations. By utilizing a distributed electric propulsion system, Zero Gravity is attempting to address safety concerns through redundancy. However, the reliance on estimated performance metrics and the lack of specific battery technology details suggest the project remains in the conceptual development phase. The success of the 2028 service entry target will likely depend on the progress of the CAAC certification process and the maturation of the aircraft's autonomous flight systems.
