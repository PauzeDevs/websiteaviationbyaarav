Zero Gravity Unveils ZG-T6 Tiltrotor eVTOL Concept

The Chinese manufacturer targets a 2028 entry-to-service for its six-passenger electric aircraft designed for urban mobility and cargo operations.

AVIATION BY AARAV NEWSROOM · 2026-11-02 · 1 SOURCE · SINGLE-SOURCE ANALYSIS

At a glance

Zero Gravity Aircraft Industry has introduced the ZG-T6, a tiltrotor electric vertical takeoff and landing (eVTOL) aircraft. The company aims for a 2028 service entry, with certification currently being pursued through the Civil Aviation Authority of China (CAAC).

Design and Configuration

The ZG-T6 is engineered as a tiltrotor eVTOL, utilizing a distributed electric propulsion (DEP) system. The airframe features a carbon fiber composite cabin designed to accommodate one pilot and five passengers, offering panoramic visibility through wrap-around windows. The propulsion system consists of six electric motors driving six tilt-propellers, which are strategically mounted on the aircraft's V-tail, high wing, and low forewing. This configuration is intended to facilitate both vertical takeoff and landing capabilities alongside efficient forward flight.

Performance and Technical Specifications

According to data reported by Aviation Week, the ZG-T6 has an estimated maximum takeoff weight (MTOW) of 2,500 kg (5,512 lb). The aircraft is projected to achieve a cruising speed of 250 kph (155 mph) with an estimated range of 300 km (186 miles) and a flight endurance of approximately 90 minutes. The design incorporates fixed tricycle wheeled landing gear and utilizes a fly-by-wire flight control system. While the manufacturer has indicated that the aircraft will feature battery-powered propulsion, specific details regarding the energy storage technology remain undisclosed.

Operational Goals and Safety

Zero Gravity Aircraft Industry intends for the ZG-T6 to serve both passenger transport and urban air cargo sectors. The company has stated that the implementation of DEP, which eliminates single points of failure, is intended to provide a high level of redundancy for enhanced safety. Furthermore, the manufacturer has announced plans to integrate autonomous flight capabilities into the platform. As of July 2025, these development milestones and performance figures are based on company concepts and have not been independently corroborated.

Key facts

  • The ZG-T6 is a tiltrotor eVTOL designed for one pilot and five passengers.
  • Zero Gravity Aircraft Industry is targeting a 2028 entry-to-service date.
  • The aircraft utilizes a distributed electric propulsion system with six motors.
  • Certification is being sought through the Civil Aviation Authority of China (CAAC).

What this means

The ZG-T6 represents a growing trend in the Chinese eVTOL sector, focusing on multi-mission utility for both cargo and passenger transport. By utilizing a six-rotor tiltrotor configuration, the manufacturer is attempting to balance the requirements of vertical lift with the aerodynamic efficiency needed for a 300 km range. However, the reliance on battery technology—for which specific energy density details are currently absent—remains a critical variable in achieving the stated 90-minute flight time and performance estimates.
