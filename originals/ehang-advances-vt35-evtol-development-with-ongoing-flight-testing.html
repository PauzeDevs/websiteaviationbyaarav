EHang Advances VT35 eVTOL Development with Ongoing Flight Testing

The Chinese manufacturer moves toward a December 2025 public debut for its new two-seat autonomous lift-plus-cruise aircraft.

AVIATION BY AARAV NEWSROOM · 2026-11-02 · 1 SOURCE · SINGLE-SOURCE ANALYSIS

At a glance

EHang has completed the assembly of its VT35 prototype, an autonomous electric vertical takeoff and landing aircraft. The company is currently conducting flight tests ahead of a scheduled public demonstration later this year, leveraging its prior certification experience with the CAAC.

Development and Certification Outlook

According to a report by Aviation Week, EHang has finalized the assembly of its VT35 prototype, which is currently undergoing flight testing. The company plans to conduct its first public flight of the aircraft in December 2025. EHang intends to pursue certification for the VT35 through the Civil Aviation Administration of China (CAAC). The manufacturer anticipates that the certification process for this model may be accelerated, drawing upon the regulatory experience gained during the successful certification of its earlier EH216-S model.

Design and Propulsion Architecture

The VT35 is designed as a lift-plus-cruise eVTOL capable of carrying two passengers in a cabin equipped with panoramic windows. Its propulsion system utilizes distributed electric propulsion (DEP), where each fan is powered by an independent electric motor. The aircraft features eight lift propellers mounted on tandem wings—four oriented upward and four downward—to facilitate vertical takeoff and landing. For horizontal cruise flight, the vehicle employs an aft-mounted pusher propeller. EHang claims that this configuration eliminates critical points of failure, thereby enhancing onboard safety through system redundancy.

Technical Specifications

The VT35 is characterized by a maximum takeoff weight (MTOW) of 2,094 lbs (950 kg) and a payload capacity of 440 lbs (200 kg). The aircraft measures approximately 26 feet in both length and wingspan. While specific performance metrics such as cruising speed, altitude, and endurance remain undisclosed, the manufacturer has indicated a projected range of 124 miles (200 km). The aircraft is designed for advanced autonomous flight control, though details regarding the landing gear configuration remain unconfirmed, as previous iterations utilized fixed tricycle wheeled gear.

Key facts

  • The VT35 is an autonomous, two-seat lift-plus-cruise eVTOL.
  • EHang plans a public flight demonstration for the VT35 in December 2025.
  • The aircraft features a distributed electric propulsion system with eight lift propellers and one pusher propeller.
  • The VT35 has a projected range of 124 miles and a payload capacity of 440 lbs.

What this means

EHang is positioning the VT35 to benefit from the regulatory pathway established by its EH216-S program. By utilizing a lift-plus-cruise configuration, the company is diversifying its portfolio beyond the multi-rotor design of its previous flagship. The reliance on DEP suggests a focus on safety through redundancy, which will be a critical factor in the upcoming CAAC certification process.
