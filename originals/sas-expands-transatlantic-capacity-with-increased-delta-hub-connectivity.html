SAS Expands Transatlantic Capacity with Increased Delta Hub Connectivity

Scandinavian Airlines bolsters its summer 2027 schedule with increased frequencies to Atlanta and Seattle, deepening its strategic partnership with Delta Air Lines.

AVIATION BY AARAV NEWSROOM · 2026-11-11 · 1 SOURCE · SINGLE-SOURCE ANALYSIS

At a glance

SAS has announced a significant expansion of its summer 2027 flight schedule, focusing on increased capacity to key Delta Air Lines hubs in the United States. The move, reported by Simple Flying, underscores the carrier's ongoing integration into the SkyTeam alliance.

Strengthening Transatlantic Frequencies

Scandinavian Airlines (SAS) is set to increase its service frequency to two major United States hubs as part of a broader summer 2027 network expansion. According to a report by Simple Flying, the airline will upgrade its Copenhagen (CPH) to Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport (ATL) route from five weekly flights to a daily service, marking a 40% increase in capacity. Additionally, the carrier will boost its Copenhagen to Seattle route from four to five weekly flights, representing a 25% growth in frequency. These adjustments are intended to restore previous service levels while enhancing connectivity for passengers traveling between Scandinavia and North America.

Strategic Alignment with SkyTeam

The expansion follows SAS's transition from the Star Alliance to SkyTeam, a move that has facilitated closer operational ties with Delta Air Lines and Air France-KLM. Simple Flying reports that the partnership between SAS and Delta has evolved significantly since its formal inception in September 2024. The collaboration, which initially focused on a revised codeshare agreement covering 150 North American and 50 Northern European destinations, was further expanded in April 2025 to include additional connecting opportunities and reciprocal loyalty benefits. SAS leadership has characterized these developments as essential for providing seamless travel options across the alliance network.

Broader Network Enhancements

Beyond the adjustments to the Atlanta and Seattle routes, SAS plans to implement a comprehensive update to its summer 2027 schedule. The airline intends to increase capacity across seven intercontinental routes in total, supported by the deployment of larger Airbus aircraft. The carrier's broader strategy includes more than 20 route enhancements across Europe and Scandinavia, alongside the introduction of two new destinations. While SAS continues to operate daily nonstop services to other Delta hubs, including Boston, Los Angeles, and New York-JFK, these routes are currently scheduled to remain at their existing frequencies.

Key facts

  • SAS is increasing Copenhagen-Atlanta service to daily flights and Copenhagen-Seattle service to five weekly flights for summer 2027.
  • The airline is expanding capacity across seven total intercontinental routes.
  • SAS officially joined the SkyTeam alliance in September 2024.
  • The partnership with Delta Air Lines includes reciprocal loyalty benefits and extensive codeshare agreements.

What this means

The decision by SAS to prioritize capacity growth at Delta hubs suggests a strategic shift toward leveraging the SkyTeam network to capture transatlantic market share. By aligning its flight schedule with the operational footprint of its primary US partner, SAS is likely aiming to improve load factors and streamline passenger transfers, thereby maximizing the utility of its recent alliance transition.
