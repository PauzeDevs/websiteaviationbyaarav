Air New Zealand Restores Direct Christchurch-Perth Link

The carrier resumes seasonal nonstop service between New Zealand's South Island and Western Australia after a five-year hiatus.

AVIATION BY AARAV NEWSROOM · 2026-11-28 · 1 SOURCE · SINGLE-SOURCE ANALYSIS

At a glance

Air New Zealand is set to relaunch its direct flight service between Christchurch and Perth on November 30, 2026, utilizing Boeing 787-9 aircraft to meet seasonal travel demand.

Resuming a Strategic Connection

Air New Zealand is preparing to re-establish its nonstop connection between Christchurch (CHC) and Perth (PER), marking the first time the airline has operated this specific city pair since 2019. According to a report by Simple Flying, the service is scheduled to commence on November 30, 2026, and will run on a seasonal basis through May 7, 2027. This route serves as a significant addition to the airline's network, specifically targeting travelers in the South Island who previously had to navigate through major hubs to reach Western Australia.

Operational Details and Capacity

The service will operate three times weekly on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays using Boeing 787-9 aircraft. Flight NZ239 is scheduled to depart Christchurch at 4:45 PM, arriving in Perth at 5:45 PM. The return leg, NZ240, departs Perth at 9:15 PM and arrives in Christchurch the following morning at 7:15 AM. Simple Flying reports that this expansion will introduce approximately 37,000 additional seats to the market during the Southern Hemisphere summer season, complementing the airline's existing year-round service from Auckland.

Market Context and Competition

Christchurch Airport remains the only facility in the South Island capable of accommodating widebody aircraft, making it the primary gateway for this long-haul service. Air New Zealand will face competition on this route, as Jetstar also maintains a seasonal presence between the two cities. The resumption of the Perth route follows a broader expansion strategy for Christchurch, which includes the recent addition of services to Singapore and Tokyo Narita, as well as successful seasonal flights to Rarotonga.

Key facts

  • Service resumes November 30, 2026, after being terminated in 2019.
  • Flights will operate three times weekly using Boeing 787-9 aircraft.
  • The seasonal schedule runs through May 7, 2027.
  • The route adds 37,000 seats to the Southern Hemisphere summer market.
  • Air New Zealand faces competition from Jetstar on this route.

What this means

The resumption of the Christchurch-Perth route indicates a strategic focus by Air New Zealand on leveraging Christchurch's unique infrastructure as the South Island's only widebody-capable airport. By providing a direct link, the airline is positioning itself to capture regional demand that avoids the congestion of Auckland's primary hub, while simultaneously competing with low-cost carriers like Jetstar for seasonal traffic.
