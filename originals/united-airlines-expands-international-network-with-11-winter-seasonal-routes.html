United Airlines Expands International Network with 11 Winter Seasonal Routes

The carrier is leveraging five major US hubs to bolster its international presence through March 2027.

AVIATION BY AARAV NEWSROOM · 2026-11-04 · 1 SOURCE · SINGLE-SOURCE ANALYSIS

At a glance

United Airlines has introduced or resumed 11 international routes as part of its IATA winter schedule, which runs from October 2026 through March 2027. The expansion includes a mix of returning services and brand-new market entries across its hub network.

Strategic Winter Expansion

According to a report by Simple Flying, United Airlines has officially transitioned to its winter schedule, effective October 25, 2026, and continuing through March 27, 2027. Data from Cirium Diio indicates that the airline will maintain an average of 350 daily international departures during this period, representing a 2% increase over the previous winter season. This growth is supported by the addition of 11 specific international routes originating from five of the carrier's US hubs.

New and Resumed Connectivity

The expansion includes both the relaunch of previously served destinations and the inauguration of entirely new markets. For instance, service from Washington Dulles to Los Cabos resumed on October 25, 2026, utilizing 737 MAX aircraft, marking a return to a route last operated in 2019. Similarly, Houston Intercontinental to Tuxtla Gutiérrez resumed on October 27, 2026, via SkyWest E175 operations. Among the new market entries, United is launching service to Sapporo from San Francisco on December 11, 2026, using the Boeing 787-9, and is establishing new connections to Cartagena from both Houston and Washington Dulles starting December 17, 2026.

Operational Scope and Exclusions

The reported 11 routes focus on international destinations beyond the US border. Simple Flying notes that certain other flights, such as the Newark to St. Croix service, were excluded from this count because the destination is located within the US Virgin Islands and is classified as domestic. Additionally, temporary charter-style operations, such as the three flights scheduled from Las Vegas to Tokyo Narita for the Consumer Electronics Show in January 2027, were not included in the primary list of 11 routes.

Key facts

  • United Airlines' winter schedule runs from October 25, 2026, to March 27, 2027.
  • The airline is averaging 350 daily international departures, a 2% increase from the previous winter.
  • The expansion includes 11 routes across five US hubs.
  • New routes include San Francisco to Sapporo and Houston to Cartagena.

What this means

United Airlines is utilizing its hub-and-spoke model to strategically increase its international footprint during the peak winter travel season. By balancing the resumption of legacy routes with the introduction of new markets, the carrier is optimizing its fleet—ranging from regional E175s to long-haul 787s—to capture specific seasonal demand, particularly in Latin American and Asian markets.
