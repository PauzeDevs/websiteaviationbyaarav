Airbus Unveils 2027 A220‑300 High‑Density Variant and Interior Refresh

Simple Flying reports that Airbus will introduce a 160‑seat A220‑300 in 2027 and will offer a new Airspace cabin upgrade for both new deliveries and retrofits, though the information remains uncorroborated by other outlets.

AVIATION BY AARAV NEWSROOM · 2026-11-28 · 1 SOURCE · SINGLE-SOURCE ANALYSIS

At a glance

According to Simple Flying, Airbus plans to launch a higher‑density A220‑300 configuration in 2027, featuring 160 seats, alongside a comprehensive cabin upgrade called Airspace. The upgrade includes lighter, larger overhead lockers, expanded LED lighting, and will be available as a retrofit for existing operators. The article also revisits the A220’s origins from the Bombardier CSeries and notes the absence of any announced A220‑500 model.

Airspace Cabin Upgrade

Simple Flying details that all A220 deliveries from this year will receive Airbus’s Airspace interior, aligning the narrow‑body’s cabin feel with that of the A350, A330neo and A320neo families. The redesign promises extra‑large overhead lockers that are roughly 20% lighter and provide more than 15% additional baggage space. Ambient lighting will be upgraded to a 16‑million‑color LED system, allowing airlines to customise illumination for branding or different phases of flight. Airbus also intends to market this cabin package as a retrofit option for operators with earlier‑generation A220s.

160‑Seat A220‑300 Planned for 2027

The same source states that beginning in 2027 Airbus will offer a higher‑density version of the A220‑300, configured for 160 seats. This layout is positioned as a revenue‑boosting alternative that avoids the need for a larger aircraft on routes where capacity is constrained. Simple Flying notes that while the higher‑density model is confirmed, the long‑rumoured A220‑500 variant has not been announced and appears unlikely in the near term.

Program Background and Production Outlook

Simple Flying recaps the A220’s evolution from the Bombardier CSeries launched in 2008. The CS100 first flew on 16 September 2013, received Canadian certification in December 2015 and entered service with SWISS in July 2016. The CS300 followed with its maiden flight on 27 February 2015, certification in July 2016 and service entry with airBaltic in December 2016. Airbus acquired a majority stake in July 2018, rebranded the family as the A220, and established a second final‑assembly line in Mobile, Alabama, complementing the original Mirabel, Quebec facility. The publisher suggests that the new interior and high‑density variant are part of Airbus’s strategy to improve profitability and production rates while offering operators greater flexibility.

Key facts

  • Airbus will offer a 160‑seat A220‑300 configuration starting in 2027
  • All new A220s this year will receive the Airspace cabin upgrade with lighter overhead lockers and 16‑million‑color LED lighting
  • The Airspace interior will be available as a retrofit for existing A220 operators
  • No A220‑500 variant has been announced according to the source

What this means

If the announced changes materialise, airlines could gain a cost‑effective way to increase capacity on thin routes without moving to larger aircraft, potentially improving unit economics. The cabin refresh aligns the A220’s passenger experience with Airbus’s larger families, which may aid brand consistency for carriers operating mixed fleets. However, the impact will depend on airline adoption of the higher‑density layout and the pricing of the retrofit package, details that remain undisclosed.
