Second B-21 Raider Test Aircraft Completes Maiden Flight

Northrop Grumman and the U.S. Air Force reach a significant milestone as a second flight-test airframe takes to the skies over Palmdale.

AVIATION BY AARAV NEWSROOM · 2026-11-02 · 1 SOURCE · SINGLE-SOURCE ANALYSIS

At a glance

The B-21 Raider program has expanded its flight-test capabilities with the successful maiden sortie of a second airborne test aircraft, marking a key advancement in the development of the next-generation bomber.

Flight Test Milestone in Palmdale

On September 11, 2025, a second B-21 Raider flight-test aircraft successfully completed its first flight in Palmdale, California. The event, which occurred nearly 22 months after the initial flight of the first test airframe in November 2023, was documented by witnesses who observed the aircraft flying with its landing gear retracted, accompanied by a Lockheed Martin F-16 chase plane. This development arrives just ahead of the 10th anniversary of the original contract award to Northrop Grumman.

Strategic Implications for the Raider Program

According to Air Force Secretary Troy Meink, the addition of a second flight-test aircraft allows the program to accelerate evaluations of mission systems and weapons capabilities. This expansion is intended to bolster the strategic deterrence and combat effectiveness of the B-21 platform. The aircraft are assembled at the Air Force’s Plant 42 complex in Palmdale before transitioning to Edwards Air Force Base for more intensive testing.

Current Status of the B-21 Fleet

The B-21 program currently involves multiple airframes in various stages of readiness. Beyond the two flight-test aircraft, Northrop Grumman has delivered two additional fully assembled airframes to the Air Force specifically designated for ground-based static and fatigue testing. Furthermore, reports indicate that two additional flight-test aircraft have been in assembly since at least February 2022, suggesting a steady progression in the fleet's development pipeline.

Key facts

  • The second B-21 flight-test aircraft completed its maiden flight on September 11, 2025.
  • The first B-21 flight-test aircraft conducted its initial sortie on November 10, 2023.
  • Northrop Grumman has delivered two additional airframes for static and fatigue testing.
  • Assembly of B-21 aircraft takes place at Building 401 on Site 4 of the Air Force’s Plant 42 complex.

What this means

The introduction of a second flight-test asset indicates that the B-21 program is transitioning from initial airworthiness verification to more complex systems integration. By utilizing multiple airframes, the Air Force and Northrop Grumman can parallelize testing, which is essential for meeting the rigorous requirements of a modern strategic bomber program.
