What this means

The Simple Flying review paints a picture of a carrier that excels in schedule reliability yet struggles with infrastructure and policy issues that can erode passenger satisfaction. The on‑time award signals operational strength, but the chronic congestion at Manila’s hub may offset that benefit by creating bottlenecks and a perception of inefficiency. Moreover, the mandatory onward‑ticket rule adds an extra procedural hurdle, potentially deterring transit passengers. Finally, the planned high‑density A350‑1000 layout could compromise comfort on flagship routes, risking a mismatch between the airline’s premium branding and the actual cabin experience. Together, these factors suggest PAL must balance its operational accolades with tangible improvements in hub capacity, customer‑friendly policies, and cabin design to sustain its reputation.
