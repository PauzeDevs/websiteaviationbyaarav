AutoFlight Unveils V5000 Matrix eVTOL for Regional Operations

The newly revealed 10-seat hybrid-electric aircraft aims to expand the operational scope of vertical take-off and landing technology.

AVIATION BY AARAV NEWSROOM · 2026-11-02 · 1 SOURCE · SINGLE-SOURCE ANALYSIS

At a glance

AutoFlight Europe GmbH has introduced the V5000 Matrix, a high-capacity eVTOL aircraft designed for regional travel. The aircraft, which has already commenced flight testing, features a hybrid-electric powertrain and a cabin design that includes a lavatory, marking a departure from typical short-range urban air mobility configurations.

Development and Flight Testing

According to a report by Aviation Week, AutoFlight Europe GmbH unveiled the V5000 Matrix in February 2026. The aircraft is a lift-plus-cruise hybrid-electric or all-electric vertical take-off and landing (eVTOL) platform. The manufacturer has already initiated flight testing, with the aircraft successfully completing a remotely piloted transition from thrustborne hover to wingborne cruise. While industry observers have speculated that the aircraft could enter service between 2027 and 2028, these claims have not been independently corroborated or confirmed by the manufacturer.

Technical Specifications and Design

The V5000 Matrix is designed to operate at the upper limits of current regulatory frameworks, boasting a maximum take-off weight of 5,700kg for the hybrid version. It is configured to accommodate up to 10 occupants, with flexible interior options including a 6-seat VIP layout or a 9-seat business arrangement plus a pilot. Notably, the design incorporates a lavatory, a feature not previously seen in eVTOL aircraft, which suggests the manufacturer is targeting longer flight durations than those typical of current urban air mobility models. The airframe is an evolution of the earlier Prosperity I and CarryAll cargo models, featuring a high-wing configuration, panoramic windows, and skid-style landing gear.

Market Positioning and Certification

AutoFlight is positioning the V5000 Matrix for the regional market, moving beyond the short-hop urban transit focus of many existing eVTOL designs. In addition to passenger configurations, the company is developing an uncrewed cargo variant. Certification efforts are currently directed toward the Civil Aviation Administration of China (CAAC), with the aircraft intended to be certified under powered-lift regulations.

Key facts

  • The V5000 Matrix is a 10-seat eVTOL aircraft with a maximum take-off weight of 5,700kg in its hybrid configuration.
  • The aircraft features a lavatory, marking a first for the eVTOL sector and signaling a focus on longer-range regional flights.
  • Flight testing is underway, with the aircraft having successfully demonstrated a transition from hover to cruise flight.
  • AutoFlight is seeking certification from the Civil Aviation Administration of China (CAAC) under powered-lift regulations.

What this means

The V5000 Matrix represents a strategic shift in the eVTOL sector toward regional connectivity. By incorporating amenities like a lavatory and increasing passenger capacity, AutoFlight is attempting to bridge the gap between traditional short-range air taxis and regional aviation. The success of this model will likely hinge on its ability to meet stringent powered-lift certification requirements while maintaining the efficiency benefits of its hybrid-electric powertrain.
