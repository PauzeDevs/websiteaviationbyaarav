U.S. Military Awards Significant Logistics and Technology Contracts

A review of defense procurement activity from March 6 to March 10, 2017, highlights major investments in KC-10 engine support and atmospheric sensing technology.

AVIATION BY AARAV NEWSROOM · 2026-11-02 · 1 SOURCE · SINGLE-SOURCE ANALYSIS

At a glance

Aviation Week reported on a series of U.S. military contract awards issued during the second week of March 2017, involving the Army, Air Force, and Navy. These agreements cover diverse requirements ranging from specialized atmospheric prediction systems to comprehensive engine logistics for the KC-10 tanker fleet.

Air Force KC-10 Logistics Support

The U.S. Air Force awarded Kelly Aviation Center LP a contract valued at approximately $1.002 billion. This indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity agreement focuses on contractor logistic support for the KC-10 Extender engine fleet. According to the report, the scope of work is extensive, encompassing engine teardown, complete overhaul services, and on-wing support provided by field teams. The contractor is also responsible for the supply chain and operational requirements, including labor, specialized tools, materials, and transportation logistics, managed through the Air Force Life Cycle Management Center at Tinker Air Force Base.

Army Atmospheric Sensing Initiative

On March 6, 2017, the U.S. Army awarded a $49.5 million order-dependent contract to Blue Storm Associates Inc., operating as Pemdas Technologies and Innovation. The contract is designated for the development and implementation of an Atmospheric Sensing and Prediction System. Aviation Week noted that the solicitation process for this project was conducted via the internet, resulting in a single bid submission. The project is being overseen by the U.S. Army Contracting Command based in Research Triangle Park, North Carolina.

Naval Aviation Support

The U.S. Navy finalized a cost-plus-fixed-fee contract with Avian LLC, based in Lexington Park, Maryland. Valued at $11.4 million, the agreement is intended to provide technical and operational support for the Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division. While the provided source material is truncated regarding the specific nature of the division's requirements, it confirms the award was processed during the specified reporting window.

Key facts

  • Kelly Aviation Center LP secured a $1.002 billion contract for KC-10 engine logistics.
  • Blue Storm Associates Inc. received a $49.5 million contract for an Atmospheric Sensing and Prediction System.
  • Avian LLC was awarded an $11.4 million contract to support the Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division.
  • All reported contract details are based on information published by Aviation Week on March 15, 2017.

What this means

The contract awards reflect a strategic focus on maintaining legacy airframe readiness through long-term logistics partnerships, as evidenced by the billion-dollar KC-10 engine support deal. Simultaneously, the Army's investment in atmospheric sensing suggests an ongoing prioritization of environmental data integration for tactical operations. The limited number of bids for the Army contract may indicate a highly specialized niche market for the requested technology.
