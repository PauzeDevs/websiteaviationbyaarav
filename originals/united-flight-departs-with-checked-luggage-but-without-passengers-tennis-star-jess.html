United flight departs with checked luggage but without passengers, tennis star Jessica Pegula says

Jessica Pegula took to social media after a United Airlines flight left the gate with her bags aboard despite the aircraft being cleared for departure without her and other passengers on board.

AVIATION BY AARAV NEWSROOM · 2026-11-03 · 1 SOURCE · SINGLE-SOURCE ANALYSIS

At a glance

According to Simple Flying, United Airlines cancelled a flight, kept passengers on the plane for two hours during rain, then reinstated the flight and took off while the checked bags remained on board. Tennis professional Jessica Pegula posted on X demanding an explanation, noting that the incident added to a frustrating travel experience en route to the WTA Finals in California. United has not yet responded, and the exact flight number and route have not been disclosed.

Incident timeline as reported

Simple Flying recounts that the flight in question was initially cancelled, after which passengers were instructed to disembark and retrieve their luggage. The aircraft remained on the tarmac for roughly two hours while a downpour continued. During that interval, passengers reportedly remained seated on the plane. After the weather passed, the airline reversed its earlier decision, re‑activating the flight and departing with the checked bags still secured in the cargo hold. Jessica Pegula, who was traveling westward from Florida toward the WTA Finals in Indian Wells, California, posted on X that the airline had "canceled our flight, told us to go to baggage claim, and then uncanceled the flight and took off with our bags."

Passenger reaction and public outcry

Pegula’s post, which used collective language such as "we" and "us," suggests that multiple travelers experienced the same sequence of events. She described the situation as "the craziest thing I've ever seen" and expressed frustration over being left on the aircraft for an extended period while the weather persisted. The incident quickly gained attention on social media, prompting discussions about passenger rights and airline operational practices during irregular operations.

Operational and security considerations

The Simple Flying article notes that U.S. regulations permit checked baggage to be loaded onto a flight even if the passenger is not present, a practice that dates back to historic security concerns. Modern procedures include safeguards intended to prevent misuse of unattended luggage, but the incident highlights how procedural lapses can create confusion and perceived security risks. The report does not provide details on whether United followed standard protocols for baggage handling in this case.

United Airlines' response and next steps

Simple Flying states that the outlet has reached out to United Airlines for comment and is awaiting a statement. No official response from the carrier has been published at the time of writing. The airline’s handling of the situation, including the decision to depart with the bags still on board, remains unverified beyond the account provided by Pegula and the outlet's reporting.
