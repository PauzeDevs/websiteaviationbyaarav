Program Overview and Eligibility

According to Simple Flying, Endeavor Air operates a Career Advancement Program that creates a defined route for its pilots to join Delta Air Lines. The key eligibility criterion is the completion of 24 months serving as a captain on Endeavor’s fleet. Once that milestone is reached, pilots may be considered for a Delta position without undergoing a separate interview or additional testing, according to the source. The article frames the arrangement as a clear career objective for first officers seeking long‑term placement with a major U.S. carrier.
