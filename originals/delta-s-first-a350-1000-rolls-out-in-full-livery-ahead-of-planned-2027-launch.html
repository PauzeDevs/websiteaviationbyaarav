Delta’s First A350‑1000 Rolls Out in Full Livery Ahead of Planned 2027 Launch

Simple Flying reports that the inaugural Airbus A350‑1000 for Delta Air Lines has been photographed at Airbus’ Toulouse plant, marking a visual milestone before the carrier’s expected June 2027 entry into service.

AVIATION BY AARAV NEWSROOM · 2026-11-10 · 1 SOURCE · SINGLE-SOURCE ANALYSIS

At a glance

Simple Flying observed Delta Air Lines’ first A350‑1000 at Airbus’ Toulouse assembly line, painted in the airline’s livery. The aircraft, identified as serial number 841, is the first of a 20‑plane order announced in January 2024. While the exterior paint is complete, the jet still requires engine installation, ground checks and flight testing before delivery, which Delta’s media kit projects for early 2027 with a commercial debut slated for June 2027. No independent confirmation of these details is available.

Visual Confirmation at Toulouse

A photograph shared by aviation outlet Airways Magazine, credited to spotter Eurospot, shows the Delta‑branded A350‑1000 positioned on the production floor of Airbus’ Toulouse facility. The image, first posted on 9 November 2026, displays the aircraft’s full exterior paint scheme, providing the public’s initial glimpse of the wide‑body in Delta colours. Simple Flying notes that the aircraft’s serial number is 841, aligning with Airbus tracking data for the first unit of Delta’s order.

Order Background and Expected Role

Delta announced a firm order for 20 A350‑1000s in January 2024, becoming the first U.S. carrier to commit to the largest variant of Airbus’ A350 family. According to Simple Flying, the airline intends the new type to augment long‑haul capacity, cargo volume and premium cabin offerings. The carrier’s media kit lists early 2027 as the start of deliveries, while industry commentary points to a June 2027 commercial launch, positioning the aircraft as a flagship for hub‑to‑hub and intercontinental routes.

Remaining Production Milestones

Simple Flying emphasizes that the painted exterior does not equate to readiness for delivery. The aircraft must still undergo engine installation, comprehensive ground checks and a series of flight‑test campaigns before Airbus can hand it over to Delta. These steps are standard for new‑type deliveries and are expected to conclude in time for the early‑2027 delivery window cited by the airline.
