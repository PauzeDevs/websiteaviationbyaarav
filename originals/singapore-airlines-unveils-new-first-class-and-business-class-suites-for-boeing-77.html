Singapore Airlines Unveils New First‑Class and Business‑Class Suites for Boeing 777‑9

The carrier announced a S$2 billion‑plus cabin refresh that includes larger suites, upgraded IFE and Starlink Wi‑Fi, with rollout expected from the second half of next year across its 777‑9 and select A350 fleet.

AVIATION BY AARAV NEWSROOM · 2026-11-06 · 1 SOURCE · SINGLE-SOURCE ANALYSIS

At a glance

Singapore Airlines revealed a comprehensive cabin upgrade at a Marina Bay Sands event, introducing new first‑class and business‑class suites for the upcoming Boeing 777‑9. The program, budgeted at more than S$2 billion (US$1.5 billion), also upgrades the airline’s Airbus A350‑900 fleet, adds a next‑generation KrisWorld entertainment system with over 2,500 titles, 4K touchscreens and OLED displays, and will bring Starlink satellite Wi‑Fi to its A350‑900, A350‑900ULR and A380 aircraft. The refresh is slated to begin in the second half of next year.

Event and Programme Scope

Simple Flying reported that Singapore Airlines staged a launch at the Marina Bay Sands Exhibition Centre, unveiling the design of its new first‑class and business‑class suites intended for the Boeing 777‑9. The airline described the upgrade as the next phase of a total passenger‑experience refresh, allocating more than S$2 billion (approximately US$1.5 billion) to the effort. While the 777‑9 launch was the headline, the airline indicated that the same cabin concepts will be retrofitted onto selected Airbus A350‑900 aircraft, extending the refresh across multiple wide‑body types.

Cabin Design and Seating

According to the Simple Flying article, the new first‑class suite is marketed as the "largest double bed in commercial aviation," though specific dimensions were not disclosed. Business‑class seats will also receive a redesign, though details on seat width or pitch were omitted. The rollout is expected to start in the second half of next year, with the airline planning to apply the new suites to the 777‑9 fleet and to some A350‑900s, but exact aircraft numbers were not provided.

In‑flight Entertainment and Connectivity

The report highlighted a next‑generation KrisWorld IFE system that will eventually host more than 2,500 entertainment options. Features include 4K high‑resolution touchscreens, OLED displays on selected cabins (Business Class on retrofitted A350‑900, First and Business Class on retrofitted A350‑900 ULR, and Premium Economy and Economy on certain 777‑9s), spatial audio, Bluetooth pairing for personal headphones, and new wellness and personalisation tools. Additionally, Singapore Airlines will introduce high‑speed Starlink satellite Wi‑Fi on its Airbus A350‑900, A350‑900 ULR and A380 aircraft, enabling streaming and real‑time communication for passengers.

Financial Commitment and Timeline

Simple Flying noted the S$2 billion-plus investment as part of a broader modernization agenda that follows the airline’s recent award as the 2026 SkyTrax Best Airline. The timeline outlined in the article suggests that the cabin refresh will commence in the second half of the upcoming year, with the first installations likely on the Boeing 777‑9 before extending to the A350 fleet. No definitive launch dates or production schedules were disclosed.

Key facts

  • Singapore Airlines announced new first‑class and business‑class suites for the Boeing 777‑9
  • The cabin‑upgrade programme is budgeted at over S$2 billion (US$1.5 billion)
  • Next‑generation KrisWorld IFE will feature >2,500 titles, 4K touchscreens and OLED displays on select aircraft
  • Starlink satellite Wi‑Fi will be installed on A350‑900, A350‑900 ULR and A380 wide‑bodies
  • Cabin refresh rollout is slated for the second half of next year and will also apply to some A350‑900s

What this means

The announced refresh positions Singapore Airlines to maintain its premium brand image amid intensifying competition in the long‑haul market. By investing heavily in larger suites, high‑resolution entertainment and satellite connectivity, the carrier aims to differentiate its product offering, especially on the new Boeing 777‑9 platform that promises higher efficiency. Extending the new cabins to existing A350‑900s suggests a strategy to elevate the overall fleet experience without waiting for the 777‑9 to enter service. The inclusion of Starlink Wi‑Fi aligns with industry trends toward broadband connectivity, potentially attracting business travelers who value uninterrupted connectivity.
