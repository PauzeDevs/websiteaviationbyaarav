United Airlines Debuts 'Elevated' Polaris Interior on Boeing 787 Fleet

The carrier's second-generation business class suite introduces privacy doors and expanded cabin configurations as part of a broader fleet modernization strategy.

AVIATION BY AARAV NEWSROOM · 2026-11-10 · 1 SOURCE · SINGLE-SOURCE ANALYSIS

At a glance

United Airlines is rolling out its 'Elevated' business class interior on its Boeing 787-9 aircraft, featuring enhanced privacy suites, larger entertainment displays, and updated cabin layouts.

Evolution of the Polaris Product

United Airlines is transitioning to the second generation of its Polaris business class product, branded as 'Elevated.' According to a report by Simple Flying, this interior upgrade is designed to address increasing passenger demand for premium, comfortable long-haul travel as aircraft capabilities allow for longer, more fuel-efficient routes. The airline has integrated these suites into its Boeing 787-9 fleet, with plans to equip all future 787 deliveries with this configuration by 2027.

Suite Design and Cabin Configuration

The 'Elevated' interior, manufactured by Adient Ascent, emphasizes privacy and versatility. Each suite features high walls and sliding doors to create a secluded environment. The cabin layout includes 32 suites, with specific configurations for solo travelers and companions. Notably, the front row introduces four 'Polaris Studios' that include companion seating. Middle seats are equipped with privacy screens, and the cabin layout alternates between seats facing the aisle and those angled toward one another to provide passengers with varied seating options.

Technology and Passenger Amenities

Beyond the physical seat, which converts into a lie-flat bed, United has focused on technological integration. The suites feature 27-inch OLED monitors, which Simple Flying reports are the largest seatback screens currently offered by a US carrier. Additional amenities include wireless charging capabilities and complimentary inflight WiFi. The airline has also updated its soft product, introducing a new caviar service and curated wine pairings to complement the hardware upgrades.

Operational Deployment

The first Boeing 787-9 aircraft featuring the 'Elevated' interior has already entered service, with initial routes connecting San Francisco to London and Singapore. United Airlines intends to scale this offering significantly, with a target of receiving 30 Boeing 787 aircraft equipped with the new interior by the end of 2027. This rollout represents a strategic effort to standardize the premium experience across the airline's widebody fleet.

Key facts

  • United Airlines' 'Elevated' interior is the second generation of its Polaris business class product.
  • The suites feature 27-inch OLED entertainment screens and sliding privacy doors.
  • The front row of the cabin includes four 'Polaris Studios' with companion seating.
  • United expects to have 30 Boeing 787 aircraft with the new interior by 2027.

What this means

The shift toward sliding doors and larger entertainment screens reflects a broader industry trend of prioritizing passenger privacy and personal space in premium cabins. By offering varied seating configurations—such as inward-facing and aisle-facing options—United is attempting to cater to both solo business travelers and leisure passengers traveling in pairs, thereby maximizing the utility of the cabin footprint.
