Press Conference Remarks

According to Simple Flying, President Trump raised the issue during a White House press conference on Friday, linking recent nationwide air‑traffic disruptions to what he described as an "ancient" U.S. ATC system. He asserted that, prior to taking office, his private pilot had relied on the air‑traffic control infrastructure of another country while operating in U.S. airspace. Pilot’s Public Rebuttal

Journalist Brian Karem, who identifies himself as a private pilot, responded on the social platform X, contesting the feasibility of the president’s claim. Simple Flying reports Karem’s statement: "You cannot call up ATC in Europe and say, 'Hey give me a hand. I'm flying over US airspace.'" He clarified that the distinction lies between the authority that controls airspace and the navigation equipment used by pilots, implying that foreign ATC cannot legally direct aircraft within U.S. jurisdiction. Karem’s rebuttal was framed as a correction to the president’s narrative rather than a personal attack.
